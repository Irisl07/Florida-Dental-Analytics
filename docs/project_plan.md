# Florida Dental Location Analytics

## Business Problem

For an experienced general dentist with approximately four years of clinical experience and growing interest in implant dentistry, which regions of Florida offer the strongest combination of income potential, professional growth opportunities, patient demand, and quality of life?

## Project Objective

Develop a data-driven framework for evaluating dental practice opportunities across Florida.

## Key Factors

### Financial

- Dentist Income
- Average Rent
- Cost of Living

### Market Demand

- Population Growth
- Senior Population
- Economic Growth

### Competition

- Dentist Density
- Number of Dental Offices

### Professional Development

- Implant Potential
- High-Value Procedures
- Long-Term Practice Growth

## Final Output

Dental Opportunity Score

Rank Florida cities and regions according to overall career opportunity.
## Target Dentist Profile

This project focuses on an above-average general dentist with approximately four years of clinical experience.

The target dentist is assumed to be proficient in:

- Root canal treatment
- Surgical extractions
- Crowns
- Complex implant procedures
- High-production general dentistry

The dentist is assumed to be seeking associate GP or DSO opportunities with strong income potential, ideally above $400,000 annually.

## Housing Assumption

Housing analysis focuses on safe, cost-effective areas within approximately 30 minutes of the target work location.

The housing benchmark is a 2-bedroom rental suitable for a couple.
