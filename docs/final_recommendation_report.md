# Florida Advanced GP Career Analytics

## Executive Summary

This project evaluates Florida dental markets for advanced implant-focused general dentists.

Target profile:

- 4 years experience
- Strong endodontics
- Surgical extractions
- Implant placement
- Goal income: $500,000+

The objective is to identify markets that provide the strongest combination of:

- Affluent patients
- Implant demand
- Practice opportunity
- Long-term career growth
