# Executive Summary

This project developed a decision-support framework for advanced implant-focused general dentists evaluating Florida practice locations.

The analysis integrated:

- Demographic indicators
- Income potential
- Senior population concentration
- Implant demand
- Competition factors
- Practice opportunity indicators

A custom Advanced GP Opportunity Score was created to compare Florida markets.

Results suggested that Naples, Sarasota, Boca Raton, Port St. Lucie, and Fort Myers offer particularly strong environments for advanced implant-focused dentists pursuing $500,000+ annual income goals.

The framework demonstrates how data analytics can support complex career and business decisions.
