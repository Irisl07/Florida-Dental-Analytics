# Business Recommendations

## Recommendation 1
Prioritize markets with strong purchasing power rather than population size alone.

## Recommendation 2
Evaluate practice environment in addition to demographics.
Procedure freedom and implant opportunity may significantly impact income potential.

## Recommendation 3
Consider long-term career trajectory.
Markets that support implant and full-arch development may generate greater long-term opportunity than markets optimized for traditional restorative dentistry.

## Recommendation 4
Use scenario-based decision models rather than relying on a single ranking.
