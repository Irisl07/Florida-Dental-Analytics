# Practice Opportunity Framework

## Objective

Evaluate whether a dental practice environment supports the development of a high-producing implant-focused GP.

---

## Procedure Freedom

Can the GP perform:

- Endodontics
- Surgical Extractions
- Implant Placement
- Bone Grafting
- Full Arch Procedures

Score: 1-10

---

## Implant Opportunity

Availability of:

- Implant Cases
- Immediate Implant Cases
- Full Arch Cases

Score: 1-10

---

## Technology

Practice utilizes:

- CBCT
- Intraoral Scanner
- Guided Surgery
- Digital Workflow

Score: 1-10

---

## Production Environment

Practice encourages:

- Comprehensive Treatment Planning
- High Value Procedures
- Case Acceptance

Score: 1-10

---

## Support Infrastructure

Practice provides:

- Treatment Coordinator
- Implant Coordinator
- Strong Hygiene Program

Score: 1-10
