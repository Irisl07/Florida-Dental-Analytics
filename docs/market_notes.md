# Market Notes

## Naples

High-income retiree market.
Strong implant potential.
High concentration of affluent patients.

## Fort Myers

Growing retiree population.
Good implant demand.
Moderate living cost compared to Naples.

## Melbourne

Lower living cost.
Less competitive than South Florida.
Strong long-term value.

## Boca Raton

Affluent patient base.
High cosmetic and implant demand.
High competition.
