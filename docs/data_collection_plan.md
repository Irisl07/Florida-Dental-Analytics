# Data Collection Plan

## Market Demographics

Variables:

- Population
- Population Growth
- Senior Population %
- Median Household Income

Purpose:

Measure long-term patient demand and purchasing power.

---

## Housing

Variables:

- Safe Area 2BR Rent

Purpose:

Estimate realistic living costs for a professional couple.

---

## Competition

Variables:

- Dentist Density
- Number of Dental Offices

Purpose:

Estimate market saturation.

---

## Advanced GP Opportunity

Variables:

- Implant Demand Potential
- Procedure Freedom
- Production Opportunity

Purpose:

Estimate likelihood of achieving $500k+ income.

---

## Technology Environment

Variables:

- CBCT Availability
- Digital Dentistry Adoption
- Advanced Implant Workflow

Purpose:

Evaluate long-term professional development potential.
